# Errata & Confirmed Corrections

**Book:** *How to Become an AI Engineer*, First Edition (2026–2027), by Mikaël Meyer, MSc.

This page lists corrections to the printed text: anything in the book that turns out to be wrong, unclear, or misstated. Changes that merely overtook the book after publication — a fee that moved, a programme that closed, a certification that launched — are not errata; they are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## How this page is maintained

Entries are listed by chapter. Each entry states:

- **What the book says** (with the entry code, for directory entries — for example CERT-10, UN-11, ON-13)
- **The corrected fact**, verified against the provider's own published information
- **The source and the date** it was checked

Nothing is posted here on the strength of a secondary source alone. If you believe you have found an error, please open an issue in this repository with your format (Kindle or Paperback), the chapter and section or entry code, and a link to the official page if you have one. Confirmed reports are credited in the entry.
