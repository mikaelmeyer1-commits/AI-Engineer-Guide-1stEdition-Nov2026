This appendix reproduces Chapter 8's three plan templates in full, as reference tables. It contains no new content. The templates are structures, not syllabi: each phase names the kind of work and the evidence that closes it, and the reader fills in topics, tools, and depth from the audit grades of Chapter 8's self-assessment method and the directory chapters. Two inputs must be set before any template is used, exactly as Chapter 8 requires: **your planned weekly study hours**, and **your current audit grades** (Chapter 2's competency matrix, graded by evidence using Chapter 8's self-audit method). Phases stretch with the hours available; a phase ends when its milestone evidence exists, not when a calendar page turns.

One rule governs every template, and it is structural, not decorative: **project work never disappears, and certification entries always sit after the hands-on phases they certify.** No phase may depend on a credential whose lifecycle status (Chapter 22: *Available / Being replaced / Not yet GA / No longer available*) has not been checked at the moment of planning — and checked again before anything is booked.

## The 6-month template — foundations, first evidence

*Assumption (Chapter 8): some programming exposure and a small weekly study budget. Readers with no programming exposure take longer on the same structure — the phases do not change; the calendar does.*

| Phase | Work | Milestone (evidence that closes the phase) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Programming and data handling repair | A cleaned, documented dataset and working code in a repository |
| 2 | Core ML concepts and first models | A baseline modelling project with a defensible evaluation |
| 3 | A structured foundations course or a free official learning path, aligned to your gaps | Completion — plus a re-audit showing the target areas at *working* level |
| 4 | First portfolio project, and an optional foundational checkpoint | A finished, documented project; optionally, one foundational examination (CERT-01, CERT-02, or CERT-03), booked only after hands-on readiness |

## The 12-month template — foundations to applied practice

| Phase | Work | Milestone (evidence that closes the phase) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations, as in the 6-month template's phases 1–2 | A repository, a cleaned dataset, and a baseline modelling project |
| 2 | Structured depth — applied coursework in your gap areas | A re-audit at *working* to *independent* in the target areas; a larger second project |
| 3 | Specialisation begins — choose the strand closest to your target role: deployment, LLM applications, or ML operations | A project in the chosen strand, including deployment or API integration |
| 4 | A first professional-level step — only if the skills precede it | Optionally, one professional certification (e.g. CERT-08 or CERT-09), taken after a lifecycle check; a third documented project |

## The 24-month template — depth, specialisation, and (optionally) a degree arc

| Phase | Work | Milestone (evidence that closes the phase) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and applied practice (the 12-month template's phases 1–3) | Two to three documented projects; core areas at *independent* |
| 2 | Specialisation depth and production habits — monitoring, testing, documentation | A project maintained over time, with evidence of iteration |
| 3 | Professional consolidation | One or two professional certifications, sequenced and status-checked (from CERT-06, CERT-08, CERT-09, CERT-14, CERT-32, as fits the strand); a capstone project |
| 4 | The long-arc decision point | Decide, on current official information, whether a degree arc such as UN-02 or UN-01 fits your goals and budget; if yes, it runs as a background commitment beyond the template |
