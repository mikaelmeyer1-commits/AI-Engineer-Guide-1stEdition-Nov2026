Welcome to the online companion for *How to Become an AI Engineer*. This repository is the book's errata and update desk: the place where corrections to the printed text are posted, and where the major changes that overtake the book's facts after publication — a programme that closes, a fee that moves, a certification that launches or retires — are recorded against the official sources.

It is maintained for at least the first year after publication, and kept afterwards as the standing record of the edition.

## About the book

"AI engineer" does not name one job. One employer uses it for someone building and operating machine-learning systems in production; another, for a developer integrating AI platforms into a product; a third, for an engineer building applications around large language models. This book compares the routes by which a person becomes qualified to do that work — degrees, online programmes, and professional certifications — on one consistent, practical basis: prerequisites, content, delivery, duration, published cost, the credential awarded, and relevance to different career goals. Every directory entry rests on the provider's own published information, and the book states where each figure came from and when it was checked.

### What's inside

- **25 chapters** in seven parts — the work itself, the skills it takes, the employment market as official statistics describe it, online learning, degrees, certifications, and getting hired
- **Directories of verified entries** — online programmes, university programmes, and certifications, each with its prerequisites, cost basis, duration, and credential
- **A method, not just a list** — how to build a true total cost (Chapter 7 and Appendix E), how to evaluate a programme (Chapter 17), and how to judge a certification's value (Chapter 22)
- **12 appendices** — the master directories, a decision tree and learning plans, an ROI calculator, a competency matrix, a glossary, the full verification methodology, the official sources, and a register of retired credentials

## How to report an error

If you find something in the book that looks wrong or out of date, please **open an issue** in this repository. The most useful reports include:

- Your **format** (Kindle or Paperback) and, if you have it, the printing
- The **chapter and section**, or the entry code if it is a directory entry (for example CERT-10 or UN-11)
- **What the book says** — a short quote is enough
- **What you believe is correct**, with a link to the provider's official page if you have one

Every report is checked against the provider's own published information before anything is posted. Confirmed corrections appear in `errata.md`; facts that have simply moved on appear in `CHANGELOG.md`. Reports that cannot be confirmed against an official source are answered in the issue itself, with the reason.

## A note on dates and figures

Programmes, fees, and certifications change constantly — that is why this companion exists. In the book, every figure carries the date it was checked and the basis on which its provider publishes it (per credit hour, per term, per year, per attempt). This companion keeps the same discipline: nothing here is posted without a source and a date, and currencies are never silently converted.

## What this repository is not

- It is **not the book**. The text of *How to Become an AI Engineer* is not reproduced here.
- It is **not a course-advice service**. Questions about which route fits your own situation are what the book's method is for (Chapters 4, 5, and 8); issues here are for errors and outdated facts in the printed text.
